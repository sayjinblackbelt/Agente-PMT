# 🤖 PMT Agent — Three Agents for Career, Learning and Digital Autonomy

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) | 🇺🇸 English | [🇪🇸 Español](README.es.md)

> An Artificial Intelligence project for PMT (Preparation for the World of Work), organized into three specialized agents for career development, learning, and digital productivity.

## Three-agent architecture

### 💼 1. Career Agent
Supports young people in preparing for their first professional opportunity.

- résumé creation and review;
- skills and experience discovery;
- job-specific résumé adaptation;
- professional presentation;
- interview simulation;
- selection-process preparation;
- job requirement analysis.

**Rule:** professionalize communication without inventing information.

### 📚 2. Study Agent
Supports learning and study organization.

- study planning;
- explanations;
- exercises;
- review and learning recovery;
- projects and assignments;
- study techniques;
- assessment preparation;
- learning goals.

**Rule:** teach the student to learn instead of simply delivering answers.

### 🖥️ 3. Digital Administration Agent
Builds practical autonomy with productivity suites.

**Microsoft Office:** Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and related tools.  
**Google Workspace:** Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Forms, Gmail, Calendar and related tools.  
**LibreOffice:** Writer, Calc, Impress and related tools.

Functions include documents, spreadsheets, presentations, email, forms, files, calendars, collaboration and administrative productivity.

**Rule:** develop transferable digital skills rather than dependence on one platform.

## Shared pedagogical foundation

The three agents share principles of **Heulosophic Pedagogy**: maieutics, mental organization, self-knowledge, autonomy, reflection and responsible decision-making.

## Safety

- no fabricated experience, skills, certificates or results;
- no unnecessary collection of personal data;
- no passwords or credentials;
- no autonomous professional decisions;
- responsible AI use;
- human guidance remains important.
