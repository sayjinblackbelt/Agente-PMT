> A real project applying Artificial Intelligence to education and preparing young people for their first professional opportunity.

## 📌 About

**My First Résumé** is an educational project designed to support young people aged **14 to 17** in creating their first professional résumé.

The project uses Artificial Intelligence to support **employability, autonomy, self-knowledge, and professional communication**.

The agent was designed to help students seeking their first professional opportunity, especially **apprenticeship and entry-level youth programs**.

The goal is not simply to generate a résumé. Students are guided through a process of:

**Discover → Ask → Explore → Reflect → Organize → Confirm → Write → Review → Adapt**

## 🎯 Objective

The project helps students:

- recognize their skills;
- identify relevant experiences;
- organize education and knowledge;
- develop professional communication;
- create a clear and truthful résumé;
- adapt their résumé to different opportunities;
- use Artificial Intelligence responsibly.

## 🧠 Core principle

> **AI should not merely produce a résumé; it should help young people understand who they are, what they can do, and how to communicate that responsibly.**

The agent therefore acts as a **guide, interviewer, organizer, and reviewer**.

## 👥 Target audience

Young people aged **14 to 17** preparing for their first professional opportunity.

## 🧭 Pedagogical approach

The project incorporates principles from **Heulosophic Pedagogy**, especially:

- **Maieutics:** guiding questions that stimulate learners to construct their own answers.
- **Mental organization:** structuring thoughts and experiences before producing the résumé.
- **Self-knowledge:** identifying knowledge, experiences, and skills.
- **Autonomy:** active student participation in creating the document.
- **Reflection:** understanding what was learned and how it can be communicated professionally.

## 🛡️ Truthfulness and responsibility

> **Professionalize the language without changing reality.**

The agent must not invent experiences, courses, certificates, skills, or results. When information is incomplete, it should ask questions.

Because the audience includes adolescents, the project includes specific safeguards for **privacy, data protection, and responsible AI use**.
